## Design Context

### Users
French-speaking professionals, researchers and students registering for the Journées de Bordeaux sur la pratique de l’ergonomie, based on the supplied reference flow.

### Brand Personality
Welcoming, considered, scholarly. Make a complex registration feel calm and human.

### Aesthetic Direction
User-approved direction: warm ivory, vivid burgundy accents, muted sage, bold geometric Space Grotesk headings with DM Sans, Bordeaux riverside photography. Four clear stages and an elegant confirmation ticket.

### Design Principles
- Preserve the reference workflow and all registration categories.
- Let typography, space and one meaningful image establish hierarchy.
- Progressive disclosure for conditional meal and billing details.
- Native controls, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, mobile-first usability.
- Static preview; no data persistence, payments or submissions.
